The Pledger.io Android app is a companion to your self-hosted Pledger.io server. It lets you review your dashboard, record transactions, manage accounts and budgets, and view reports from your phone.

>[!IMPORTANT]
> The Android app does not include a Pledger.io server. [Install Pledger.io](/getting-started/installation/) first and make sure your phone can reach it.

## What you need

- A phone or tablet running Android 8.0 or newer.
- A working Pledger.io server that is reachable from your device.
- A Pledger.io username and password. Create your user in the web app if you do not have one yet.
- An HTTPS server address for the release app.

The Android app currently supports username-and-password sign-in. Two-factor and OpenID Connect sign-in are not available in the app.

## What you can do

- View net worth, recent transactions, and monthly activity.
- Add, edit, and review transactions.
- Scan an invoice or bill to prefill a transaction.
- Create, edit, and review accounts.
- Set up monthly budgets and expense groups.
- Review income, spending, categories, budget performance, net worth, and balances.
- Manage categories, tags, app appearance, language, and biometric unlock.
- Read previously loaded data when your device is offline.

Some web features are not yet available on Android, including CSV import, contract management, transaction deletion, and offline changes.

## Main navigation

Use the five tabs at the bottom of the app:

| Tab | Use it to |
|-----|-----------|
| **Dashboard** | See net worth, monthly activity, and recent transactions. |
| **Transactions** | Browse transactions by month and add or edit a transaction. |
| **Budgets** | Create a monthly budget and track expense groups. |
| **Accounts** | Review balances and manage your accounts and counterparties. |
| **Reports** | Explore income, expenses, categories, budgets, net worth, and account balances. |

Tap the settings icon in the top-right corner of any main tab to open **Settings**.

## Dashboard

The Dashboard is your starting point. It shows:

- Your net worth.
- Income and expenses for the current activity summary.
- Your most recent transactions.
- The finance experience currently in use.

Tap a recent transaction to open its details, or tap **View all** to open the full transaction list. Pull down on the page to refresh its data.

## Add something

Tap the **+** button on the Dashboard to choose an action:

- **New transaction** records income, an expense, or a transfer.
- **Scan invoice/bill** uses a photo to prefill a transaction.
- **New account** adds a bank account, wallet, card, loan, or counterparty.

The **Transactions**, **Accounts**, and **Budgets** tabs also have a **+** button for actions related to that section.

## Search

Tap the search icon on the Dashboard and enter part of a name or description. Search can find:

- Transactions
- Your accounts
- Counterparties
- Categories

Tap a result to open it. Transaction search covers recent activity, so older transactions may not appear.

## Choose Guided or Power mode

Pledger.io offers two finance experiences. Both modes contain the same features; they change what is expanded by default.

| Mode | Best for | Behavior |
|------|----------|----------|
| **Guided** | New or occasional users | Keeps optional transaction fields collapsed and explains where to find them. |
| **Power** | Frequent users | Opens advanced fields and transaction filters, and shows templates immediately. |

To switch modes, open **Settings** → **Finance experience**.

## Refresh and loading states

- Pull down on most lists and overview pages to get the latest server data.
- Scroll to the bottom of long transaction and account lists to load more.
- If a page cannot load, tap **Retry** after checking your connection.
- When the banner **You're offline. Showing cached data.** appears, the app is using information saved on the device.

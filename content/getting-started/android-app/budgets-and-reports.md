## Set up your first budget

If you already created a budget in the web app, open **Budgets** to view it. Otherwise:

1. Open the **Budgets** tab.
2. In **Start your first budget**, choose the start month and year.
3. Enter your expected **Monthly net income** after tax.
4. Tap **Create budget**.

Pledger.io creates the monthly budget period. You can then add expense groups for the areas you want to control.

## Add an expense group

An expense group is a spending bucket with a monthly limit, such as Groceries, Bills, or Transport.

1. Open **Budgets**.
2. Tap **+** or the empty-page action.
3. Enter a name.
4. Enter the maximum **Monthly budget**.
5. Tap **Save**.

When recording an expense, select this group under **More options**. The budget card then compares classified spending with your limit.

## Review budget progress

1. Open **Budgets**.
2. Use the month controls to review the current or an earlier month.
3. Review the amount spent and budgeted on each card.
4. Tap an expense group to see its progress and remaining amount.
5. Tap **View transactions** to open the transactions assigned to that group.

Pull down to refresh the figures from your server.

## Change an expense group's limit

1. Open the expense group.
2. Tap **Edit**.
3. Change the monthly amount.
4. Tap **Save**.

The Android app cannot rename or delete an existing expense group, or change expected income after initial setup. Use the web app for budget operations that are not shown on Android.

## View reports

Open the **Reports** tab, then select a report at the top:

- **Overview** summarizes cash flow, balances, budget performance, top categories, and net worth.
- **Income vs expenses** compares money received and spent.
- **Category breakdown** shows where spending was classified.
- **Budget performance** compares spending with expense-group limits.
- **Net worth** shows how your total position changed.
- **Account balance** reviews balances for individual accounts.

Use the month controls to choose a reporting period, and pull down to refresh.

If a report says **No data for this period**, check that transactions exist in the selected month and that they have the classifications needed by that report.

Reports require a connection to load current results. A recently opened report may remain visible briefly if the connection drops, but reports are not stored for long-term offline use.
